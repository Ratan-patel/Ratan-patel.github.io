REFUND & CANCELLATION GUIDANCE

Confirm the delivery boundary before paying.

Manual access and professional services need clear, written expectations. This page explains the baseline approach; any accepted proposal or enrollment confirmation may contain additional terms.

COURSES

Request changes before access delivery

Ask questions about format, availability and materials before payment. Cancellation or refund requests should be emailed before cohort access or digital-material delivery; each request is reviewed against what has already been delivered.

DIGITAL MATERIALS

Delivery matters

Once downloadable or private digital materials have been delivered, they may not be refundable except where access was not supplied as confirmed. Keep the purchase confirmation and transaction reference.

SECURITY SERVICES

Proposal terms control

VAPT, red-team and consulting engagements are scope-first. Any deposit, cancellation, rescheduling, retest and final-payment terms are set in the written proposal or engagement letter before work begins.

No automatic renewal claim

Current manual UPI access is a confirmed first-month payment, not an automatic recurring subscription. Do not assume a renewal unless you receive a written confirmation for the next access period.
