
## Google SEO guidance

Google Search Central says there is no guarantee that a page will be indexed, but following Search Essentials improves eligibility. Its starter guide emphasizes descriptive URLs, unique helpful people-first content, appropriate title links and meta descriptions, crawlable links, canonicalization when duplicate URLs exist, and submitting a sitemap through Search Console. It also warns against keyword stuffing and says the keywords meta tag is not used for ranking.

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide

## Additional distribution research

Paddle's official AUP help page is a relevant compliance checkpoint for software sales, but an OS image containing security tooling should be submitted for merchant review before listing. The safe positioning is a downloadable Linux training/assessment environment with written authorization requirements, not an illegal-access or offensive-service product.
