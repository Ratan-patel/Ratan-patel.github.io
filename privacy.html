PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Share only what is needed to start a safe conversation.

The public website is designed to route inquiries through your mail client, email or WhatsApp rather than a hosted contact-form database. Treat all security conversations with care.

WHAT YOU MAY SHARE

Inquiry details

Name, organization, business email, the type of request, approximate asset counts and non-sensitive scoping context are usually enough for an initial discussion.

WHAT NOT TO SHARE

Secrets stay out of inquiries

Do not send passwords, API keys, UPI PINs, OTPs, full bank statements, production customer data, exploit payloads, unrestricted system access or sensitive credentials through a public inquiry.

PAYMENT PROOF

Manual confirmation only

If payment proof is requested for a confirmed purchase, share only the minimum UPI UTR or USDT ERC-20 blockchain TX hash and, if needed, a redacted screenshot. Never expose a UPI PIN, OTP, seed phrase, private key or complete account history.

THIRD-PARTY CHANNELS

Email, WhatsApp and external tools

Messages sent through email, WhatsApp, GitHub Pages, the external RATAN AI application or external download hosts are also subject to those providers’ own terms and privacy practices.

RATAN AI caution: avoid pasting production secrets or personal data into an AI assistant. Use sanitized, synthetic or test-case examples unless a separate written data-handling arrangement exists.
RATAN AI V5 chat: the browser sends chat messages to the configured server-side proxy, which forwards them to the Gemini API. The Gemini API key is not sent to or stored in browser JavaScript. Conversation retention, access-code handling and provider processing depend on the final proxy deployment configuration; do not use the chat for confidential data.
