What every engagement includes

DELIVERY

Evidence your team can use

  • Executive summary with risk themes and priorities.
  • Technical findings with reproducible, non-destructive evidence.
  • Practical remediation guidance and stated limitations.
  • Report readout for relevant technical stakeholders.
  • Retest availability where the written package includes it.
BOUNDARIES

Control before testing

  • Written authorization, asset inventory and scope owner are required.
  • No destructive testing, outage testing or data extraction without explicit approval.
  • No test is started merely from a contact-form request or direct access.
  • Only the systems, accounts and time window listed in the approved rules of engagement are in scope.
  • Do not send production credentials, secrets or customer data in an initial inquiry.

Frequently asked questions

Is payment required for access?

No payment is required. VAPT and red-team work is scope-first; a discovery conversation and written authorization come before free access.

Will you test production?

Only when the written rules of engagement permit it and the agreed safeguards are in place. Staging is preferred where it meets the objective.

Can you guarantee that every vulnerability will be found?

No security assessment can give that guarantee. The report documents the agreed scope, methods, observations and limitations so your team can make an informed decision.
