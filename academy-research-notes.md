
## Official course research

- MIT xPRO Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity: https://xpro.mit.edu/courses/course-v1:xPRO-PCCYx/ . Official page lists a 24-week online program and topics including threat landscape, incident response, protective strategies, vulnerability identification/testing, simulated web-application attacks, SOCs, cloud, IoT/OT and AI in cyber. Use only as an inspiration/reference, not as a claim that the user's Academy is affiliated with MIT.
- Harvard University, CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity: https://pll.harvard.edu/course/cs50s-introduction-cybersecurity . Official page lists a free, self-paced, 5-week introductory course covering hacking/cracking, social engineering/phishing, passwords, brute-force/dictionary attacks, MFA and password managers. Use as a public reference link and do not imply Harvard certification for the user's course.

- Oxford University MSc in Software and Systems Security: https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-software-and-systems-security . Official page describes malware, forensics, cloud platforms, governance, secure programming, network security, risk analysis, cloud security, cyber threat intelligence and a project/dissertation. This is a graduate systems-security reference, not an ethical-hacking certificate.
- Oxford Department of Computer Science Advanced Security: https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/teaching/courses/2024-2025/advsec/ . Official page covers attack detection, malicious threats, attack vectors, vulnerabilities, intrusion detection, threat modelling, pentesting, incident response, deception, cyber threat intelligence, SOCs, CSIRTs and ethical considerations. It is a useful inspiration for a defensive/operations track, but the listed page is a 2024-2025 academic course page.
