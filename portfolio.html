Attach the CV, one page, text-based PDF (ATS parses it; the old image-PDF did not).
Log the date in the tracker, then follow up once after 4 working days on the same thread.
Apply on the portal too where one exists (Kratikal, Aujas, TAC, Amity) — email gets attention, portal gets you into the system.
