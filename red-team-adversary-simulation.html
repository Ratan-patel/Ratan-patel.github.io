Curriculum

The program teaches planning, controlled emulation, detection feedback and decision-quality reporting.

WEEK 01 // GOVERNANCE

Scope, ROE and success criteria

Build an exercise that executives, defenders and operators can all understand.

  • Rules of engagement and stop conditions
  • Asset, identity and data boundaries
  • Success metrics and safety gates
  • Lab: write the operation brief
WEEK 02 // INTELLIGENCE

Threat intelligence and ATT&CK mapping

Turn a threat narrative into testable hypotheses.

  • Adversary profile and capability mapping
  • Technique selection and assumptions
  • Detection coverage questions
  • Lab: build a technique matrix
WEEK 03 // PLANNING

Campaign design and communications

Coordinate an exercise without confusing a real incident.

  • White-cell, blue-team and operator roles
  • Escalation paths and emergency stop
  • Evidence and event timeline design
  • Lab: rehearsal and inject plan
WEEK 04 // ACCESS

Initial-access simulation

Model entry paths safely using synthetic scenarios.

  • Phishing-awareness and exposed-service narratives
  • Control validation without real delivery
  • Assumption tracking and safe proof
  • Lab: evaluate a mock entry path
WEEK 05 // IDENTITY

Identity and privilege boundaries

Test whether defenders can detect risky privilege transitions.

  • Role graphs and trust relationships
  • Least privilege and approval paths
  • Detection-aware scenario design
  • Lab: map a synthetic identity path
WEEK 06 // OPERATIONS

Operational discipline and OPSEC

Keep the simulation observable, reversible and accountable.

  • Operator logs and decision points
  • Change control and rollback planning
  • Evidence minimization
  • Lab: run a controlled timeline
WEEK 07 // PURPLE TEAM

Detection feedback and remediation

Convert an exercise into measurable defensive improvement.

  • Alert quality and analyst observations
  • Coverage gaps and false-positive review
  • Remediation ownership and deadlines
  • Lab: purple-team review board
WEEK 08 // CAPSTONE

Executive-ready adversary report

Defend the campaign design and its business conclusions.

  • Timeline, findings and evidence limits
  • Risk narrative for leadership
  • Prioritized improvement roadmap
  • Final briefing and certificate review

Operating model

01 // Plan

Define the threat narrative, scope, roles, stop conditions and success measures.

02 // Simulate

Use synthetic assets and controlled lab steps with a named white-cell owner.

03 // Observe

Capture defender decisions, detections, timing and communication quality.

04 // Improve

Deliver a prioritized roadmap and retest the agreed defensive controls.
